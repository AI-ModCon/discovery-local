  Dear Participant,
  We’re thrilled you’re joining us for the Genesis Mission: Hands-on Hackathon held at Argonne National Laboratory on October 14–16, 2026.
  The Genesis Mission: Hands-on Hackathon is designed with one clear outcome in mind: every Genesis Mission RFA Award team leaves with a strong starting point for integrating their scientific workflow into the Genesis Mission Platform. Featured sessions will cover critical foundational, intermediate, and advanced topics. Each session will start with a short tutorial, then will prioritize hands-on work on your specific problem set. Technical experts will be available for the entire three days to help drive your success.
  Facilitators and support staff from ModCon and the AmSC will be on-site during the entire Hackathon to provide support to the RFA Award Teams. Please consider your team’s goals for the Hackathon and seek out information and support as appropriate for your team.

  AmSC Accounts: Please follow the instructions here to request access to AmSC. This will be the mechanism that provides access to the Model Access Gateway (MAG) and other AmSC services. Once your AmSC access is enabled, you will need to join “Genesis Mission October 2026 Hackathon” via myAmSC to gain access to a dedicated pool of tokens for the event. Questions about AmSC should be submitted here.
  ESnet (AWS): ESnet provides cloud compute on AWS (CPU and NVIDIA GPU nodes with shared storage), accessible through the IRI-API. Users can use it to run containerized jobs and manage files without a facility account or a batch queue wait; it is a quick way to test that a workflow is portable across facilities or to run jobs when LCF queues are busy. Teams can show and test their workflow is portable across facilities and use cloud resources to overflow when LCF queues are too busy, time-sensitive jobs, or data that already lives in AWS.
  ESnet East is an IRI endpoint hosted on Amazon Web Services. It accepts your AmSC identity directly, so you don't need a separate ESnet account. To access it, log in to MyAmSC (https://my.amsc.energy.gov/) and request to join the AmSC project AMSC082 (Genesis-Mission-October-2026-Hackathon). Once you're a member, select Genesis-Mission-October-2026-Hackathon in the left sidebar, open Personal Access Tokens, click New Token, and copy the token. It is shown only once and is valid for 30 days. Use it as a Bearer token with the ESnet IRI API at https://iri-dev.ppg.es.net/api/v2.
